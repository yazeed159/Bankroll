⚙️ Rules & setup
Players can borrow from the bank (up to the limit) and repay later at this interest rate, from the Manage panel on their turn.
🤝 Alliances
Once this is on, pair players up from the lobby list below (each pair shares one pooled balance — money either of them earns or spends comes out of the same pot — and the whole pair goes bankrupt or wins together).
🏠 Rent
⏳ Turn timer
Shown as a shrinking ring around the active player's avatar. If time runs out, their turn is ended automatically (or the dice are rolled for them if they haven't rolled yet). The clock pauses whenever a player is stuck negative and can't legally end their turn — it picks back up once they're square again.
🎲 Side bets
A purely social side-game — anyone other than the active player can challenge a quick $25/$50/$100 bet on their upcoming roll (doubles, odd/even, or over/under 7) right up until they roll. Winner takes the wager straight from the loser; it never touches properties, rent, or the bank.
🔨 Auctions
Owners can also start their own auction on properties they own (no houses, unmortgaged) from the Manage panel, picking their own starting bid and a 3/6/9s timer.
